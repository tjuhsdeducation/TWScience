🍎 For Teachers

Students choose one of these teachers when they sign in, and their scores go to that teacher’s own Google Sheet:

  • Everything is autograded. Each station is scored as the student works (data tables, sorts, drop-downs, quizzes, and the CER). Their total is sent when they press Submit.
  • Your Google Sheet (“Bio Lab Scores - your name”) has one tab per lab. Each tab shows every period, sorted by last name, with color-coded percents and a ⬇ Download for Aeries button.
  • Preview mode lets you click through any lab without signing in. Nothing is sent.
⚠️ This site isn’t connected to Google Sheets yet, so scores are only saved on each student’s device. See README.md to finish setup.
